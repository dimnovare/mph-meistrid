# MPH Meistrid OÜ — Logo & Mini Brand Identity

Create a professional logo and small visual identity for:

**MPH Meistrid**

MPH Meistrid OÜ is an Estonian building, renovation and finishing company.

The identity should communicate:

- craftsmanship
- reliability
- precision
- construction
- practical experience
- strength
- trustworthy workmanship

The customer is not a technology company.

The logo needs to feel appropriate on:

- a construction van
- workwear
- building signage
- invoices
- quotations
- website
- social media
- favicon

---

# Brand Direction

Create something:

**strong + simple + modern + memorable**

Avoid over-designing it.

The identity should feel like a serious construction company rather than a cheap handyman service.

Aim for a visual identity that could still look current 10 years from now.

---

# Logo Structure

Explore several concepts based around:

**MPH**

and

**MPH MEISTRID**

The letters MPH should ideally form the distinctive visual element.

Explore whether geometry associated with building can be incorporated subtly into the lettering itself:

- structural lines
- blocks
- beams
- corners
- walls
- floor plans
- construction geometry
- precision
- modular forms

The construction connection should ideally be embedded into the identity rather than added as a separate cliché icon.

---

# Avoid

Do NOT default to:

- generic house outline
- roof over the company name
- hammer + wrench crossed together
- hard hat icon
- construction worker clip art
- skyscraper stock icon
- generic building-company template
- overly detailed emblem
- gradient-heavy logo
- 3D effects

Avoid anything that looks like a cheap logo marketplace template.

---

# Typography

Use bold, highly legible typography.

Consider:

- geometric sans serif
- industrial proportions
- strong uppercase lettering
- confident spacing

The word:

**MEISTRID**

can act as a secondary descriptor beneath or beside MPH.

It must remain readable when printed on a vehicle or work jacket.

---

# Colour

Propose 2–3 practical colour systems.

Prefer colours associated with construction and professionalism.

Possible directions worth exploring:

### Direction A

Charcoal / near-black  
Warm construction orange

### Direction B

Dark graphite  
Safety yellow / warm yellow

### Direction C

Deep dark navy / graphite  
Concrete grey  
Strong accent colour

Do not automatically use blue corporate branding unless it genuinely produces the strongest identity.

Colours must work in:

- full colour
- black
- white
- monochrome

---

# Website Compatibility

The identity will be used on a clean modern construction-company website.

The logo should work particularly well in:

- navigation header
- hero
- mobile header
- footer

It should not require a large amount of empty space around it.

---

# Explore Concepts First

Before selecting the final identity, present several clearly different directions.

For example:

### Concept 1 — Architectural MPH

Construct the letters from architectural/structural geometry.

### Concept 2 — Master Mark

A strong custom MPH monogram that works particularly well on workwear and vehicles.

### Concept 3 — Precision Construction

More minimal and premium, using exact geometric cuts inspired by construction drawings.

### Concept 4 — Bold Contractor

Very direct, masculine and strong, optimized for immediate recognition from a distance.

Do not make all concepts variations of the same logo.

---

# Evaluate Every Concept

Evaluate each direction for:

- readability
- memorability
- professionalism
- construction relevance
- small-size readability
- vehicle branding
- workwear
- web use
- monochrome reproduction

Choose the strongest direction and refine it.

---
