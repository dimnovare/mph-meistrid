1Four directions for MPH Meistrid
1aArchitectural MPH — chosen
MEISTRID
MODULAR BEAM CONSTRUCTION
Every letter is assembled from one beam width with a visible joint gap, the way walls read on a plan. Square counters keep it legible when it shrinks; the accent marks the load-bearing member.
1bMaster mark — interlocked monogram
MEISTRID
SHARED STEMS · ONE CONTINUOUS BEAM
The three letters share stems and one horizontal beam runs through the whole monogram. Extremely strong as a badge or embroidery, but the P asks the eye to work harder than a wordmark should.
1cPrecision construction — drawing lines
MEISTRID
OUTLINE + DIMENSION LINE
The same modular letters drawn as parts on a technical drawing. Premium and precise on a quotation, but the hairlines close up on vinyl, embroidery and at favicon size.
1dBold contractor — distance first
MPH
MEISTRID
TYPE-LED BLOCK · CUT CORNER
Heaviest and loudest: readable across a car park and cheap to reproduce. It is also the least distinctive — the block and cut corner do the work, not the letters, so it dates faster.
